# Interview Demo Walkthrough

## Goal

Demonstrate that enterprise learning effectiveness should connect trustworthy assessment evidence to capability and operational outcomes.

## 1. Open Outcome Chain

Say:

> “I deliberately separate completion, assessment evidence, capability mastery, operational adoption, and business outcomes. They answer different questions.”

Point out that completion is much higher than adoption.

## 2. Explain the decision consequence

Say:

> “If completion is 96% but adoption is closer to half the target, I would not automatically assign another course. I would investigate workflow friction, manager reinforcement, incentives, tooling, and whether the learning actually addressed the barrier.”

## 3. Open Item Intelligence

Find `Q09`.

Say:

> “This item is deliberately flawed. Its negative item-rest relationship tells me stronger performers are not behaving as expected on the item. That's a review trigger, not an automatic deletion rule.”

## 4. Open Capabilities

Compare mastery with adoption.

Say:

> “This is where assessment stops being an isolated quiz report. Items roll into role-relevant capabilities, and I can compare demonstrated knowledge with actual operational behavior.”

## 5. Open Transfer Gaps

Say:

> “These learners completed the required learning but still have weaker mastery or adoption. That is a materially different population from somebody who simply never completed training.”

## 6. Open Data Lineage

Say:

> “The analytics service doesn't become the owner of everything. LMS owns completion and assessment responses; CRM owns operational adoption; enterprise data owns downstream outcomes. The analytics layer creates governed insight from those signals.”

## 7. Open Swagger

Show `/api/items`, `/api/capabilities`, `/api/cohorts`, and `/api/data-lineage`.

## Closing line

> “My objective is to move L&D reporting from activity metrics toward trustworthy evidence and measurable capability outcomes without overstating causality.”
