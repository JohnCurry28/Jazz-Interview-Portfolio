Decision Insights

Why this matters

High completion does not prove capability.
High assessment performance does not prove adoption. When mastery is healthy but workflow adoption lags, the next intervention may be manager reinforcement, UX/workflow remediation, better job aids, or incentive/process changes rather than another course.

Statistical evidence supports expert review. It should not be treated as automatic proof that an item, learner, or program is valid or invalid.
