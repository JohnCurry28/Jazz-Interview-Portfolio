# Metrics Reference

## Item Difficulty

`p = correct / N`

Higher values indicate easier items.

## Upper/Lower 27% Discrimination

`D = P_upper - P_lower`

The cohort is ranked by total assessment score. The upper and lower 27% groups are compared.

## Item-Rest Correlation

Pearson correlation between the item score and the learner's score on all other items.

## KR-20

`KR20 = k/(k-1) * (1 - Σ(pq)/variance(total))`

Used here because the sample is dichotomously scored.

## Capability Score

`correct mapped items / mapped items`

## Capability Mastery

The MVP counts a capability as mastered when at least two of its three mapped items are correct.

## Overall Learner Mastery

`mastered capabilities / total capabilities`

## Completion

Synthetic LMS completion signal.

## Adoption

Synthetic CRM/workflow behavior signal.

## Business Outcome Index

Synthetic downstream operational-quality signal.

## Correlations

The demo reports mastery/adoption and adoption/business correlations. They are descriptive and should not be interpreted causally.

## Review Rule Reminder

Psychometric thresholds are decision-support heuristics. They are not substitutes for SME review, assessment validity evidence, or governance.
